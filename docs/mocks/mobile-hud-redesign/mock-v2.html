The fix that matters. Today the dev row and build buttons are fed the current player, so during three bot turns they swap to the bot's cards and the bot's affordability every 700 ms — that churn is the blinking. Here they stay yours and only dim.

The fan, precisely

Cards overlap by 22 px of a 54 px card, and z-index runs 5 → 1 left to right, so each card covers the left edge of the one after it. Count badges sit bottom-right, which is the part that stays exposed — all five numbers are readable at once. Five cards occupy 182 px, leaving the stack and its label the right half of the row. A resource you hold none of keeps its slot as a dashed card showing 0, so the fan never re-flows as your hand changes.

Where the side margin actually was

Reducing the stage padding was not the win — it was already 3 px. The margin came from the board's own viewBox, which enclosed ~18% empty space around the hex cluster. Re-cropping it to the cluster's bounds plus a deliberate 3% margin took the painted hexes from 314 px wide to 366 px — a 17% linear increase with no layout change at all. Cropping flush to the hexes would have reached 384 px, but the board then touches both screen edges with nothing to breathe against; 3% (11 px a side) keeps the frame deliberate while giving back most of the width.

Caveat for the build. This mock draws no harbours. The real board's bounds include the port markers out at sea, so cropping this tight would clip them. In the app the equivalent change is to compute the frame from the ports' outer bounds rather than a fixed pad — same effect, harbours intact. Expect a slightly smaller gain than 22% once they are accounted for.

Fixed-slot rules this layout commits to

  • Every control is always mounted. Roll does not swap out for the build buttons — it dims to "Rolled ✓" in place. Trade and End turn dim rather than disappear.
  • The dock's three rows have fixed heights (grid-template-rows:74px 62px 50px), so the board's height is a constant and never re-measures mid-turn.
  • The dock always shows you, never the active player. Hand, cards and build affordability read from your seat, so a bot's turn changes nothing but the dimming and the status line.
  • Zero-count resources hold their slot as dashed cards, so positions are stable from turn one.
  • Dice stay anchored to the board's bottom-right and are the one thing that appears and disappears — over the board, not in the dock, so no reflow.

Open questions

  • Tapping the stack opens the card tray as a bottom sheet — should the tray push the board up, or float over it? Floating keeps the "board never resizes" rule intact.
  • Bank supply is not in this dock at all. Proposal: move it behind the existing ? guide, since it is reference data rather than a per-turn input.
  • Public-hands mode shows four hands. Does the fan become four smaller fans, or does it open as a sheet?
  • In landscape, the three dock rows become a right-hand column of the same three groups — worth confirming before build.
